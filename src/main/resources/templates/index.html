About The Project

Bridging Citizens & Municipal Authorities

The Civic Smart Tracking System is designed to replace traditional paper-based municipal complaint processes with a modern digital governance platform.

With structured categorizations, unique tracking identifiers, and automated status workflows, municipal officials can efficiently assign, monitor, and resolve civic grievances while keeping citizens informed every step of the way.

  • Instant Complaint ID Generation
  • Real-time Resolution Status Workflow
  • Direct Administrative Oversight

Key Project Highlights

Simple & User-Friendly

Clean blue and white government theme built for ease of use by citizens of all technical backgrounds.

Full-Stack Spring Boot Architecture

Robust JPA data repositories, Spring MVC controllers, and clean HTML/Thymeleaf view rendering.

MySQL & H2 Database Support

Supports instant in-memory embedded runs out of the box and full production MySQL integration.

Municipal Services

Civic Complaint Categories

Citizens can lodge complaints in five major public service categories:

Water Leakage

Report main water pipeline bursts, street leakages, and valve leakages requiring immediate repair.

Water Supply Issue

Lodge issues related to low pressure, erratic timing, contaminated water supply, or total outage.

Drainage Problem

Report clogged sewage drains, stormwater flooding, open manholes, and sanitation overflows.

Garbage Collection

Request clearance of overflowing public bins, missed doorstep garbage collection, or illegal dumping.

Street Light Issue

Report broken street lamps, non-functional night lighting, or flickering poles in your locality.
